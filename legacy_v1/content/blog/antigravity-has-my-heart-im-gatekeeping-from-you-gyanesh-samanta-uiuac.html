So what is it?

Antigravity is an AI - IDE (Integrated development environment), you can think of it as vs code (a development environment, used by the majority of the software developers), but better.

What does it do best?

Build, ship, deploy.

A lot of you must have got on the hypetrain of Claude being better than Chatgpt, a few of you are familiar with NotebookLM (my desktop app version of NotebookLM now has over 150 downloads!) and a handful of you might be aware of claude code, or claude code with ollama or even open claw.

But then there's Antigravity. Free use to Claude opus 4.6 (yes, although rate limited, the rate by themselves are quite generous), free access to Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT OSS Medium, Claude Sonet and Gemini Flash. All within an IDE, all within an environment, where context sits within the folder that you open, be it files, docs, existing code bases. Access to models sits on the right pane, and the center is where you see the show.
